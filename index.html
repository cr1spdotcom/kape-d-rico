We don’t roast for the bag. We roast for the cup — the one in front of you, right now.

Kape d’ Rico started in 2019 with one roaster, one bar, and a short list of farms we could name from memory. Most of those farms are still on the list. The ones that aren’t were replaced slowly, after visits, never by catalogue.

We buy six origins a year — no more. Each is roasted for a single method and served until it runs out. When a coffee is gone, it’s gone. We’d rather say no than serve a guess.
